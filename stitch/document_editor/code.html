Mobile Checkout Redesign — Spec v2

Overview

The current mobile checkout experience has a 42% drop-off rate at the payment step. This redesign focuses on reducing cognitive load by consolidating shipping and billing into a single-flow interface, leveraging biometric authentication where available.

Goals

  • Reduce time-to-complete by at least 25% for returning users.
  • Introduce Apple Pay and Google Pay as primary express actions.
  • Optimize form validation to appear inline rather than at submission.
